# Policy: Data Retention

## Scope
Applies to all customer transaction records, support tickets, and
internal incident reports.

## Retention Periods
- Transaction records: 7 years (regulatory requirement).
- Incident reports: 3 years.
- Support chat transcripts: 1 year, unless flagged for a compliance
  investigation, in which case retention extends to 7 years.

## Access Controls
Transaction-level PII is classified "restricted" and is only accessible
to Analyst and Administrator roles. Aggregated/anonymized incident
metrics are classified "internal" and accessible to all roles.
